Four things
  1. 01

    AI handles the writing.
    This handles the reading.

    Preview and source, one key apart. Notion and Typora themes work as they are. Mermaid, Graphviz and math, all tuned. The whole app installs at ~15 MB, with no browser engine inside.

    A shaky line? Highlight it. A doubt? In the margin. Wrong? Fix it on the spot.

    Claude, Codex and OpenClaw all have a chat window. None of them is made for reading.

  2. 02

    The best of the last
    generation. Built in.

    local-first · git sync · outliner · [[wikilinks]] · backlinks · wiki pages · daily notes · plugins

    Roam and Obsidian worked this out years ago. note.md puts it back into files. One plugin brings your whole Roam graph over. An Obsidian vault just opens.

  3. 03

    No AI inside.
    Made for AI.

    Calls no model. Sends no request. Not one.

    Your folder is the workspace every AI tool shares. Cowork, Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT, OpenClaw, Hermes — same files, same house rules, all in git. See how.

    Every file it writes follows Open Knowledge Format v0.2, strictly: what a document is, where it came from, who checked it — plain YAML at the top of the file. Any tool can read your vault. Not just this one.

    Change AI tools whenever you want. What's yours stays yours.

  4. 04

    Need something else?
    Add it.

    Write a plugin. Add a scheduled job. Hang your skills off it.

    Put a ? in a note and an agent takes it from there: edits the document, fills in the context, hands it back — async. Whether you keep it is up to you.
